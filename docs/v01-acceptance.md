## Preserve scope and authority

Configured, CI verified, release rehearsed, published and provenance verified
remain separate states. Public signed fixtures, unsigned handoffs and synthetic
state-machine tests do not constitute a complete signed rehearsal of Armorer's
own producer/consumer flow. Native writes remain blocked by required unsupported
gates. No public release, package publication, tag movement or settings mutation
was performed by this reconciliation. L3 and optional adapters remain deferred.

The [preceding ledger](../reviews/2026-10-03-v01-acceptance-before-reconciliation.txt)
retains every original dated receipt and pending observation. Its old source
acceptance labels describe that earlier snapshot. Use this ledger and the
[source map](development-status.md) for current accepted implementation and the
remaining operational gates. Historical preview/catalog receipts remain intact.
